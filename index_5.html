Problem

Enterprise teams were cleaning complex, multi-sheet Excel workbooks by hand or with one-off Pandas scripts — slow, unrepeatable, and prone to silently breaking cell formatting, formulas, and layout.

What I built

An asynchronous, multi-tenant web platform where users upload workbooks, get automatic schema/anomaly detection, configure non-destructive cleaning rules, review flagged records inline, and export sanitized files with full formatting preserved.

Technical approach

FastAPI backend streaming uploads through io.BytesIO buffers on Starlette thread pools; dynamic type downcasting and an O(N) lookahead sniffer for fast schema inference; RapidFuzz (C++ layer) for fuzzy record matching; OpenPyXL coordinate-mapping to preserve cell styles and formulas through the clean cycle; PostgreSQL + SQLAlchemy for auth, sessions, and audit logs.

Result

Processes complex multi-sheet workbooks in under two minutes with 99.9% accuracy — a 15–20x speedup over ad-hoc scripting, with zero formatting loss and a full audit trail of every removed record.
