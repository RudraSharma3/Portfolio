About

Hi, I'm Rudra.

I'm an ML engineer at BytePX in Jaipur. I studied Computer Science with a specialisation in AI/ML at UPES Dehradun, and I've been putting my work on GitHub since 2023.

Most of what I build sits between a model and somebody's messy data: a multi-sheet workbook that has to keep its formatting, a chatbot that has to answer in Hindi and English, an ERP that disagrees with what people say they worked. I like problems where the answer can be checked, so a lot of my work is deterministic on purpose.
